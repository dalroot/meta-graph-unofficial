# Facebook Integration Module

This module documents reverse-engineered operations connecting Instagram with Facebook and Meta Account Center.

## Operations
- `CXPFbStoriesCurrentPrivacyQuery`: Controls simultaneous Instagram & Facebook Story distribution.
- `XAVSwitcherFetchFBNTAEligibilityQuery`: Validates cross-account switching eligibility between Instagram and Facebook identities.
- `FetchCXPNoticesQuery`: Pulls joint Meta terms, privacy updates, and legal notices.
